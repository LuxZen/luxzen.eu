This is the first piece in a place that has no readers yet. That isn't an
inconvenience — it is the point. Writing for an empty house forces a kind of
honesty that writing for an algorithm does not.

## Why now

I have spent years working with people and organisations that get stuck
inside their own structures. What I keep seeing is this: the people are
not the problem. The system they operate inside produces the behaviour we
then label as "their problem". This insight is not new — Donella Meadows,
Russell Ackoff and a hundred others described it long before me — but it is
an insight you have to *earn* over and over, because the surrounding culture
keeps quietly pushing it away.

These essays are my way of earning that insight again, out loud.

## What will appear here

No takes on the news cycle. No hot takes at all. Instead: slow observations
about the systems we live in — work, healthcare, education, technology,
democracy — and the patterns they share. Sometimes personal, sometimes
abstract, hopefully never predictable.

## How this place works

No analytics. No cookies. No JavaScript. No newsletter (yet). There is an
[RSS feed](/essays/index.xml) for those who still use one. The source of
this site lives on [Codeberg](https://codeberg.org/eluxzen/luxzenburg).

If something here resonates, you may tell me. If something is wrong, please do.
