My name is Erik van Luxzenburg. This place is a personal archive of essays —
not a blog in the contemporary sense, more a slow notebook growing in public.

The pieces here are written from a systems-thinking perspective: the
conviction that much of what we experience as individual problems is in
fact a property of the systems we move through.

The Dutch counterpart of this site lives at
[luxzenburg.nl](https://luxzenburg.nl/).

For my professional work and trainings, see [luxzen.eu](https://luxzen.eu/).
